# Projects Fil Rouge – Data Repository

## Overview

**Projects Fil Rouge** are long-term, integrative projects conducted by students preparing the engineering degree at SupBiotech.  
They span multiple semesters and are designed to simulate real-world biotechnology projects with increasing levels of autonomy and complexity.

This repository serves as a centralized platform to store, organize, and document the data generated throughout these projects.

---

## Objectives of Projects Fil Rouge

The Projects Fil Rouge program aims to:

- Train students in end-to-end management of complex scientific and technological projects  
- Develop advanced skills in biotechnology, data analysis, and innovation  
- Strengthen autonomy, critical thinking, and decision-making  
- Expose students to interdisciplinary approaches and real-world constraints
